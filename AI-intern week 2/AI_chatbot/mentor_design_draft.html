I’m building an image classifier. What should I check before training?

F

FieldnoteProject mentor

Start with the data before changing the model. A few checks now can prevent confusing results later:

  1. Confirm the labels match the images and check how many examples each class has.
  2. Look for unreadable files, duplicates, and unexpected image sizes.
  3. Make separate training and validation sets. If classes are uneven, preserve their proportions in the split.
  4. Keep a final test set untouched until you have finished choosing the model.

Are your images organized in folders, a CSV file, or both? I can suggest the next step for your setup.
